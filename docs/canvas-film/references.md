# Launch films behind the Canvas title study

Research reviewed on 19 November 2026. The times below identify chapters or
the beginnings of shots in a published frame index. Visual observations come
from inspecting selected frames of the original films via Cutmap. The Apple
sound observation comes from the maker’s transcript.

### Figma Slides · 26 June 2024

A frame from the opening deck overview shows rows of slides. Around 0:29, a
two-axis tone control fills the view. Around 0:34 the resulting headline is
readable back on the slide. At 1:24, colored response markers show a live poll.
The change in framing lets the viewer see both the input and what it produces.
For Demotion, bring the editable title into focus, then show the same words
on the canvas beside the folded ring.
[Figma’s film](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p-B--SGq_ZQ&t=29s) · [Indexed frames](https://cutmap.co/v/figma-presenting-figma-slides)

### Remotion · 8 February 2021

Jonny Burger’s launch trailer puts code on the right and a working preview on
the left. Frames from the shots beginning around 0:45 and 0:51 preserve that
layout while the dots in the preview change color. By 0:57, a video file sits
on the desktop. For Demotion, hold the title card steady while the playhead or
tempo changes. Finish the demonstration with the PNG that **Save frame**
actually produces.
[Maker’s trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gwlDorikqgY&t=44s) · [Indexed frames](https://cutmap.co/v/remotion-launch-trailer)

### Arc Boosts · 26 October 2022

The opening frame shows Julius introducing himself to the camera. Late in the
demo, the shot beginning near 0:58 shows the setup dialog over a website.
Around 1:03 the style editor and the recolored site share the screen. The site
remains a visual reference across the change. For Demotion, carry the same
title and ring across the draft, beat grid, and final card so the viewer can
follow one piece of work through all three stages.
[The Browser Company’s walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=53KQ2wUZG2s&t=58s) · [Indexed frames](https://cutmap.co/v/arc-introducing-boosts-with-julius)

### Apple WWDC24 · 10 June 2024

In the iPadOS chapter (43:04–50:52), the presenter describes adjusting an
angle in Math Notes and watching the graph update. Apple’s transcript also
marks music at the transition into the macOS chapter. For Demotion, a short
original cue can mark the transition to the final card. Keep the title, moving
beat indicator, and export action visible so the sequence also reads with the
sound muted. [Apple’s keynote](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RXeOiIDNNek&t=2584s) · [Apple’s transcript and chapters](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2024/101/) · [Apple’s guidance on muted previews](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-previews/)

The editable inputs, drawing, and exported frame come from the
[Demotion Canvas example](../../examples/canvas-launch.html). The linked films
provide references for showing those actions in sequence.
